4. The narrative

The report places Trump 41st with 312 points from 142 raters on a 1–10 scale across ten equally weighted traits, and that placement is confirmed against the frozen record and its double witness.

"Each of the 10 categories is"

The methodology page answers the weights question while the overall page answers rank and score, a separation corrected against the habit of citing one page for both halves.

The overall-last label belongs to the 2018 anchor story rather than this series, and anything beyond the frozen pages stays unverified in this lesson.

5. The machinery

The instrument asks each rater to grade every president 1–10 on ten traits, then weights the traits equally and sums to the overall score.

That design makes 312 a within-series total only; cross-series reading follows the convergence, never the raw points.
