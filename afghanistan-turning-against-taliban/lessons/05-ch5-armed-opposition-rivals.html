3. Narrative

The chapter opens with a cold admission. No group combines public support, fighters, money, and logistics to challenge the Taliban directly. No exile army gathers across a border, and no breakaway region runs a parallel government. That strategic doctrine is argument, unverified as record, and the deferred Syria comparison stays unverified with it.

The prescription follows from the obstacles. Loyalists in every village, secret police on every corner, punishing geography, and engineered scarcity make open war suicidal. So resistance must go small, cellular, and dispersed, living off the land and eroding the center. The grind-it-down lineage is confirmed in broad outline: both Soviet and American campaigns learned how Afghanistan punishes large formations, per the Council on Foreign Relations conflict tracker.

The National Resistance Front leads the tour. Founded within the 15 to 17 August 2021 window, heir to the old Northern Alliance, led by Ahmad Massoud from France and fought by Shahim's men inside. The founding window and the predecessor history are confirmed, the latter in broad outline against the security-analysis baseline of the Crisis Group Afghanistan work. The 10,000-fighter and 500-attack figures are NRF self-claims only, set against far lower UN tallies of 29 and 56, while the foreign-backer list stays unverified. Caption garbles in this passage are corrected throughout: every stray acronym resolves to NRF.

A Pashtun commander on a Kandahar base, elite-unit pedigree, Western training, Capitol Hill presence, and near-certain intelligence ties, now striking the secret-police infrastructure as a self-styled protector. 4. Machinery

The chapter runs four logics at once.

  • Dispersion as survival: cells too small to detect, too many to uproot.
  • Inversion as signal: a Kandahar-based front means the base itself doubts.
  • Split as opportunity: divided rulers answer every front more slowly.
  • Spoiler as arithmetic: each fighter sent against IS-K is missing elsewhere.

Every name dispute and every number in this chapter is flagged in place. The gaps register carries them until record checks land.
