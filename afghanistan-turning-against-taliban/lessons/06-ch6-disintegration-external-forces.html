Disintegration and the Outside Appetite

The recap restates the thesis as fact. Loyalty has curdled into disintegration, the social-contract pretense lies destroyed, and the people who carried the movement to power now live with hunger, thirst, and disease. Three shifts carry the disintegration case. Resistance once isolated in the north now emerges in Kandahar on Pashtun support. IS-K, once localized, strengthens toward a new wave. The movement once deemed cohesive now splits at the top while local commanders test how much they can take. Each shift is the video's account, and the southward-spread claim in particular has no second source yet.
