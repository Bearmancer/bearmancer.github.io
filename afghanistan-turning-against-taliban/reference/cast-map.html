Context and Offstage Voices

Chapters 1 through 4, 6, and 7 carry no on-stage cast: they argue through populations, institutions, and unnamed leaderships rather than named actors. Offstage voices such as the journalist Bilal Sarwari appear only as attributed sources inside chapter 5, so they hold glossary entries but no roster rows. Fronts whose identity the narration conflates, notably the NLF and the AFF, are tabled separately here until a record check settles them: chapter 5.
