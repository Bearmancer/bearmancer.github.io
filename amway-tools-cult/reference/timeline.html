How to read this page

Every entry is one date, one line of event, one colour tag for its kind, and a status in plain words. Verified means the date/event was checked against the record in a lesson or cited source (the note says which). Source-claimed means it rests on the video's account alone — asserted, no record located. Colour carries kind only, never status. Where the record corrects the video, the entry sits at the corrected date and says so.

Legend — six kinds, one colour each

  • founding & business companies founded, people joining, products, sales, fortunes
  • regulation & courts FTC complaints, decisions, lawsuits, prosecutions
  • media & publication books, records, tapes-as-publications, press exposés
  • politics presidents, resignations, rallies with candidates, attempts
  • internal Amway documents & actions memos, studies, tapes, terminations by the corporation
  • meetings & confrontations founding scenes, showdowns, rallies, parallel meetings

Status is written out, every entry: "verified — …" (with the source) or "source-claimed — …" (why it is not verified). Kind meanings stay stable as the page grows; old entries are never recoloured.

Act I — The raw material, 1936–1963

  • 1936
    media & publication Dale Carnegie publishes How to Win Friends and Influence People. Verified — lesson 0002 check.
  • 1937
    media & publication Napoleon Hill publishes Think and Grow Rich, later atop the Amway reading list. Verified — lesson 0002 check; the "con man" portrait is investigative journalism, not peer review.
  • 1939, Dec 6
    founding & business Dexter Yager born in Fulton, New York; raised in Rome. Verified — obituaries; correction: the video says "born in Rome", conflating birthplace with hometown.
  • 1945
    founding & business Mytinger & Casselberry become Nutrilite's national distributors — the multi-level selling mechanism is invented. Verified — Branchik scholarship; correction: "first MLM" is a 1945 mechanism, not a 1930s company.
  • 1946
    founding & business Doug Wead born in Indiana; lifelong Pentecostal, later Yager's ghostwriter and a tools kingpin. Source-claimed — the video's dating; no independent check in this pass.
  • 1949
    founding & business DeVos and Van Andel form Ja-Ri Corporation, their Nutrilite distributorship vehicle. Verified — corporate history; correction: the video's "Amway founded in 1949" compresses Ja-Ri into Amway (1959).
  • 1954
    meetings & confrontations The Fontainebleau Hotel opens on Miami Beach (grand opening 20 December) — site of both the founding scene (~1970–71) and the 1983 showdown. Record — the hotel's own history gives 1954; the video says 1955 (a common secondary-source error).
  • 1956
    media & publication Earl Nightingale releases the spoken-word record The Strangest Secret. Verified — lesson 0002 check; the "first Gold Record" claim stays soft (traces to his own company's marketing).
  • 1957
    media & publication Charles Manson completes the Dale Carnegie course in federal prison at Terminal Island. Verified — modern biography via lesson 0002; correction: "inspired Manson" is better told as coursework completed, techniques reused.
  • 1959 (Apr; commonly Nov 9)
    founding & business The American Way Association is incorporated; Amway Sales & Services follow — the founding of Amway. Verified — corporate history / IBOAI timeline.

Act II — Amway and the parallel empires, 1964–1969

  • 1964
    founding & business Yager joins Amway; Ja-Ri is absorbed into Amway Corporation; William Penn Patrick founds Holiday Magic on the Amway model. Verified — obituaries/corporate history for the Amway half; video's account, uncontested, for Holiday Magic's founding year.
  • ~1966
    founding & business Someone — possibly Fred Hansen — lends Yager Nightingale's Strangest Secret record. Source-claimed — the Hansen loan is unverified, and the record says the Basses, not DeVos, sponsored Hansen.
  • 1966, fall
    founding & business Yager sponsors Tony Renard into Amway; Renard buys a second-hand tape recorder and begins duplicating motivational tapes. Source-claimed — "Tony Renard" is unfindable under any spelling; oral tradition only.
  • ~1967
    founding & business Yager "applying for other jobs" within three years of joining — the video's evidence the model was already exhausted. Asserted, no record located — unsupported by any obituary or profile; the inference is the video's argument.
  • 1967
    founding & business Patrick founds Leadership Dynamics, pioneering large-group awareness training. Verified — lesson 0002 parallels check.
  • 1968
    founding & business Alexander Everett founds Mind Dynamics; Patrick later holds a stake. Verified — lesson 0002 check (Patrick bought in 1970).
  • 1969
    founding & business The Yagers move Rome → Charlotte, NC; Glenn Turner founds Dare to Be Great! in Orlando — graduated cassette courses totalling $8,000. Verified — obituaries for the move; court record for Dare to Be Great's rallies and pricing.

Act III — The tools business is born, 1970–1974

  • 1970
    founding & business Bill Britt joins Amway (sponsor: Dominic Conigliaro, beneath Yager); ex-Army officer, municipal manager in central North Carolina. Verified — obituary; correction: central NC (Raleigh/Sanford/Goldsboro/Carrboro), not "the Charlotte area".
  • ~1970–71
    meetings & confrontations The Fontainebleau coffee-shop pitch: a nameless young man offers a $50,000 tape plant; Yager declines, Britt says "I'll do it." Source-claimed — Wead's account only; the exact quote is unlocated (candidate: The Magic Makers, 1982).
  • 1971, Jan 18
    regulation & courts FTC complaint against Holiday Magic (Docket 8834). Verified — 84 F.T.C. 748.
  • 1972, May 24
    regulation & courts FTC complaint against Koscot (Docket 8888). Verified — 86 F.T.C. 1106.
  • 1972
    media & publication A book-length exposé of Mind Dynamics appears. Source-claimed — named by the video; no independent check in this pass.
  • 1973
    politics Vice President Spiro Agnew resigns in a bribery scandal; Gerald Ford succeeds him under the 25th Amendment. Verified — public record.
  • 1973
    founding & business William Penn Patrick dies; his companies close. Verified — lesson 0003 check (his death sits inside Holiday Magic's end, with state and SEC suits).
  • 1973
    regulation & courts F. Lee Bailey and Glenn Turner indicted on Koscot conspiracy charges; hung jury. Source-claimed — the video's account; no independent check in this pass.
  • 1974, Aug
    politics Gerald Ford becomes president after Watergate — the Grand Rapids congressman next door to Ada is in the White House. Verified — public record.
  • 1974, Oct 15
    regulation & courts FTC decision against Holiday Magic: cease the "open-ended, multilevel marketing plan". Verified — 84 F.T.C. 748.

Act IV — The FTC years, 1975–1979

  • 1975, Mar 25
    regulation & courts FTC complaint against Amway (Docket 9023) — the four-year battle begins. Verified — FTC docket.
  • 1975
    politics DeVos and Van Andel "visited [Ford] privately in the White House" — content unrecorded, deal (if any) unknown. Asserted, no record located — no archive entry found; the wider Ford–Amway tie is documented (conventions, Oval Office visit per Politico).
  • 1975, Sep 5
    politics Lynette "Squeaky" Fromme attempts to shoot President Ford in Sacramento. Verified — Ford Library blog for the attempt; correction: no source ties Fromme's own recruitment to Carnegie technique (Manson's was).
  • 1975, Nov
    regulation & courts Koscot final order — driven out of business as an illegal pyramid. Verified — 86 F.T.C. 1106 (month firm; the volume's OCR gives an unreliable day).
  • 1978
    media & publication Don't Let Anybody Steal Your Dream appears under Yager's byline — probably ghostwritten by Wead. Source-claimed — the video's attribution; the book's emptiness is the video's judgment.
  • 1978, Nov
    media & publication Jonestown: 909 dead in Guyana (914 with Georgetown) — the cult Kerns and Wead will co-write about. Verified — NYT, Nov 1978.
  • 1979
    media & publication Kerns and Wead publish Peoples Temple, Peoples Tomb (Logos). Verified — Open Library / SDSU Jonestown archive.
  • 1979, May 8
    regulation & courts In re Amway Corp. final order: not an illegal pyramid — but also guilty of resale-price maintenance and misleading earnings claims. Verified — 93 F.T.C. 618; correction: a limited order with operating rules, not a ringing endorsement or statutory safe harbor.
  • 1979–80 (video says "1980")
    regulation & courts Wisconsin Direct Distributors' tax returns average a net loss of $918 after business expenses. Verified — expert affidavit; correction: $918 not $900, tax years 1979–80, case filed July 1982; "almost every single one" is the video's embellishment (only the average is sourced).

Act V — The apex, 1980–1981

  • 1980, late summer
    politics Candidate Ronald Reagan speaks at a massive Yager rally in Charlotte; Wead's quickie Reagan biography is dedicated to Yager. Source-claimed — the video's account; the Wead–Reagan–Yager tie is consistent with Wead's bibliography.
  • 1980
    meetings & confrontations Free Enterprise Days: a Charlotte rally spills into the streets at 3 a.m. chanting "Five and six nights a week." Verified in substance — the chant is on the record (Detroit Free Press, 1982); the staging is the video's account.
  • 1980
    founding & business Yager's wealth portrait at its peak: the Lake Wylie mansion as pilgrimage site, Rolls-Royces, boats, jet, the "Freedom Special" motor home. Verified in substance, details adjusted — log house on Lake Wylie; 3 Rolls + 4 Mercedes; 4 boats + Hawker jet; $350k motor home (Observer series).
  • 1980 (Dec 29 per video)
    founding & business Freedom Distributing formed — the financial hub of Yager's tools empire (Internet Services Corp. from 1989; "50 states and 10 foreign countries"). Year and scale verified — Observer series / Fifth Circuit; the exact day is the video's dating.
  • 1980
    founding & business Amway's $1 billion sales year. Verified — Amway's own corporate chronicle; caution against reading 1982 as a revenue collapse.
  • ~1981
    founding & business Wead becomes an Amway distributor himself. Source-claimed — the video's "on or around 1981" dating.

Act VI — The battle, 1982–April 1983

  • 1982
    media & publication Phil Kerns publishes Fake It 'Til You Make It (Victory Press) — "the first book-length exposé" (a critic's assessment). Verified — Open Library; "first" qualified, not established fact.
  • 1982, Jul
    regulation & courts State of Wisconsin v. Amway Corp. filed (Dane County #589806); AG Craig subpoenas Directs' returns. Verified — case references; correction: filed July 1982, not "1980".
  • 1982, Aug 26
    media & publication NYT: "U.S. Disputes Amway Claims" — press heat in the terrible year. Verified — NYT archive.
  • 1982, late
    internal Amway documents & actions Amway commissions a comprehensive internal study of the tools business after the crosslining affair. Source-claimed — named at the chapter boundary; corroborated by the January memo it produced.
  • 1982, late
    internal Amway documents & actions Leonard Hall poaches distributors up and down the California coast with his own tools line — crosslining; Amway terminates his distributorship. Source-claimed — the video admits it knows "very little"; no press or court trace (a distributor wiki conflicts on dates).
  • 1982
    internal Amway documents & actions The Challenge of the 80s warns the Policy Committee the tools' illegality threatens Amway's existence. Source-claimed — named by the video; contents belong to the chapter-8 account.
  • 1982
    regulation & courts A federal district court in Ohio warns Amway on distributor compliance ("widespread illegalities inherent in Amway designed systems of tapes, books, and rallies"). Asserted, copy unlocated — the video quotes a source quoting it; the original was not found.
  • 1983, Jan 10
    internal Amway documents & actions Ed Postma (business conduct) to Patrick Sullivan (Northwest sales): the Britt/Yager analysis — "The tool business is illegal… It is a pyramid." Source-claimed — quoted at length in the video; no independent copy located in this pass.
  • 1983, Jan
    internal Amway documents & actions DeVos and Van Andel release the two Directly Speaking tapes — count Amway-approved tapes toward bonuses; the kingpins read it as a declaration of war. Source-claimed — the video's account; no independent copy located in this pass.
  • 1983, early
    media & publication 60 Minutes airs "Soap and Hope": Mike Wallace interviews Dexter and Birdie Yager, leaves the tools-vs-Amway income question open. Source-claimed — the video's account of the broadcast; not independently checked in this pass.
  • ~1983, early
    meetings & confrontations A big confrontation meeting in Hawaii — mentioned in several sources including the Miami tape; no record survives. Asserted, no record located — the video says it is "lost to history".
  • 1983, Apr 5
    meetings & confrontations The Miami Showdown: Ron Lindebaum and Bob Abraham meet the kingpins (Herrera, Hale, Wead, Dexter and Birdie Yager, others) on a Fontainebleau terrace; taped, acrimonious, cordial at the close. Source-claimed — the tape's account; no attendee roster located; archive.org copies asserted, not checked.
  • 1983, Apr 5
    meetings & confrontations Same day, same hotel: a scripted ballroom session with the lower-level "emeralds" — fewer revelations, tape incomplete. Source-claimed — the video's account.
