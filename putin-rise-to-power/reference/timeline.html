How to read this page

Every entry is one date, one line of event, one colour tag for its kind, and a status in plain words. Verified means the date/event was checked against the record in the lesson or a cited source (the note says which). Source-claimed means it rests on the video's account alone — asserted, no record located. Colour carries kind only, never status. Where the record corrects the video, the entry sits at the corrected date and says so.

Legend — five kinds, one colour each

  • posting & service Putin's KGB career steps, household dates, the recall
  • reform & policy Gorbachev's reforms and the system strain behind them
  • diplomacy treaties, summits, public non-intervention commitments
  • bloc upheaval elections, openings, crowds — the 1989 cascade and the Dresden night
  • press claim later allegations about the Dresden years, kept as allegation

Status is written out, every entry: "verified — …" (with the source) or "source-claimed — …" (why it is not verified). Kind meanings stay stable as the page grows; old entries are never recoloured.
