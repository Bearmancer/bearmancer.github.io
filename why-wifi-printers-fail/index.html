Why WiFi Printers Fail

A WiFi printer that works on Monday and vanishes on Tuesday is not haunted. Three failure layers stack inside one cheap device: a fragile multicast discovery protocol, a driver model Microsoft is now retiring, and an ink business that blocks third-party cartridges. This course walks through each layer, explains why the combination feels random, and ends with the fixes that actually work.
