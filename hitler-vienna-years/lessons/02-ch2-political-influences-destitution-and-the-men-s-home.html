Political Influences, Destitution, and the Men's Home

Hitler studied Schönerer and Lueger before falling into homelessness and moving into the Meldemannstrasse men's home.123

Political Influences in Vienna

Georg Ritter von Schönerer, the founder of the Austrian pan-German movement, was one of the politicians Hitler most admired upon arriving in Vienna.1

Vienna Mayor Karl Lueger modernized city infrastructure by municipalizing gas, electricity, and transit networks while expanding social healthcare benefits.4

Destitution and the Meldemannstrasse Men's Home

Hitler became completely destitute in the late autumn of 1909 and sought shelter at the Meidling homeless shelter.2

Hitler and Reinhold Hanisch moved into the modern municipal men's home on Meldemannstrasse in Brigittenau on February 9, 1910.3

Hitler filed a formal complaint at the Brigittenau police station on August 5, 1910, accusing Reinhold Hanisch of withholding revenue from his paintings.5

Hitler established an independent living selling his watercolor paintings directly to Jewish picture-frame and art dealers, including Jakob Altenberg and Samuel Morgenstern.5
