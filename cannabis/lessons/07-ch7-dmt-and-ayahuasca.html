DMT and ayahuasca

Ayahuasca showed short-term mood improvements in a small observational study, with no controlled proof of lasting benefit.12

The study

A study of 57 ayahuasca ceremony attendants in the Netherlands and Colombia measured mood and thinking before the ceremony, the day after, and 4 weeks later.3

Mood results

Self-rated depression and stress decreased after the ceremony and stayed lower at the 4-week check-in, in this small study of mostly healthy volunteers.1

Limits of the evidence

The evidence is limited: a small observational study without a control group, in which only 31 of the 57 participants completed the 4-week follow-up.2
