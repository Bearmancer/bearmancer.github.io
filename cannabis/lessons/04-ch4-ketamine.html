Ketamine

SPRAVATO (esketamine nasal spray) is FDA-approved for certain depression treatments, with strict safety rules.12

Approved uses

SPRAVATO is FDA-approved for treatment-resistant depression in adults, alone or with an oral antidepressant, and for depressive symptoms in major depression with acute suicidal thoughts or behavior.1

Safety rules

Because of sedation, dissociation and breathing-slowing risks, patients must be monitored for at least 2 hours after each dose, and the drug is only available through a restricted safety program (REMS).2

What it has not been shown to do

Its effectiveness in preventing suicide or reducing suicidal thoughts or behavior has not been demonstrated.3
