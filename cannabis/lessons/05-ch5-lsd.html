LSD

LSD remains a Schedule I drug, and early trial results suggest possible benefits for anxiety.12

Legal status

LSD remains a Schedule I substance, and that classification continues to hinder large-scale clinical trials and broader medical acceptance.1

Trial results

In a Phase IIb trial with 198 participants, a single 100 microgram dose produced symptom remission in 50% of those with generalized anxiety disorder.2

Safety concerns

Reported safety concerns include cardiovascular risks, emotional vulnerability, and the potential for hallucinogen-persisting perception disorder (HPPD).3
