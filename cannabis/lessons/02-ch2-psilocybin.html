Psilocybin

In one small trial, psilocybin-assisted therapy was linked to lasting improvements in depression, but the study was too small to prove it works generally.12

The trial

A randomized, waiting-list study enrolled 27 adults aged 21 to 75 with moderate-to-severe depression.1

Results at 12 months

Of the participants followed for 12 months, 75% met the study's response threshold and 58% were in remission.2

These are results from a small trial, not proof that the treatment works for depression in general.2

Safety

No serious adverse events were judged related to psilocybin during the long-term follow-up, but only 24 participants were followed.2
