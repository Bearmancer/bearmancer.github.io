MDMA

The FDA found the MDMA therapy application's safety data unreliable and its lasting benefit unproven.12

Why the FDA objected

The FDA found the application's safety data unreliable because the sponsor's training taught study sites not to report 'positive' or 'favorable' effects as adverse events.1

The agency also concluded the application did not show the treatment effect lasted beyond the 18-week end-of-study assessments.2

What happens next

The FDA recommended a new clinical trial to assess how long any benefit lasts and to properly characterize safety before approval.3
