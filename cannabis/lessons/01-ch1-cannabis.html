Cannabis

Cannabis use in young people is linked to lower school grades, but the studies show association, not proof of cause.1

School outcomes

Among adolescents and young adults, cannabis use was probably associated with lower school grades.1

The pooled studies also linked cannabis use with higher rates of school dropout and absenteeism.1

How strong is the evidence?

These were observational studies, so they cannot show that cannabis itself caused the lower grades.1

Confidence in these links is limited: 51 of the 53 studies assessed (96%) had a high risk of bias on at least one criterion.2
