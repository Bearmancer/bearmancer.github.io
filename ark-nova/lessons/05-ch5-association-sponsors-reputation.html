Association, Sponsors, Reputation

The Association Action

The Association action buys exactly one task at a time. You pay with association workers from your zoo map, and each task needs enough action strength.

Your first worker on a task costs one worker, a repeat visit costs two, and a third repeat is blocked until the break returns everyone.

Four tasks are on offer:

  • Raise reputation by 2 and take any bonus shown, at strength 2.
  • Take a partner zoo from the board, at strength 3.
  • Take a university from the board, at strength 4.
  • Work on a conservation project, at strength 5.

The rulebook gives the tasks and their strengths.

Reputation decides which display cards you can reach. Some Sponsor cards also demand a minimum reputation before you may play them.

Each partner zoo cuts 3 money per continent icon off every Animal card from its continent, and each partner zoo also counts as one icon of its continent.

Partner zoos have two hard limits. You may never hold two from the same continent, and you may never hold more than four in total.

Your third and fourth partner zoo need the upgraded Association action. The official FAQ V2 EN pins that requirement to the zoo plan rather than the card face: "No, you cannot. The requirement of having upgraded the Action card is on your zoo plan, not only on your Action card." The board supply refills at each break.

Universities raise your reputation, grant research icons, and are the only way to lift your hand limit to 5 cards. Like zoos, no duplicates, and the supply refills at the break.

Conservation Projects

Conservation projects are the thing that actually converts your zoo into a score. You either support a project already in play or play one from your hand and support it at once.

Supporting means placing one of your map tokens on an unclaimed condition you fulfil. You advance on the Conservation track, plus Reputation where shown, and you fire the bonus of the map space you took the token from.

You may support each project only once. Where you meet several conditions at once, you pick one.

Release projects work differently. You discard a matching Animal from your zoo, move Appeal back by its value, and free its enclosure, as community summaries of the Ark Nova rulebook (Capstone Games, English) also describe. The FAQ sets the order: "You lose the appeal before you gain the conservation points." (official FAQ).

The upgraded Association action changes the pace. You may perform several different tasks in one action, as long as their total value fits your strength.

Upgraded play also unlocks the donation. After at least one task, you may pay the smallest visible donation amount for 1 conservation point, once per action.

The Sponsors Action

The Sponsors action plays exactly one Sponsor card from your hand. The official FAQ states the from-hand-only rule: "No. You may only play a sponsor card from your hand." Your action strength must meet the card level, plus any conditions printed on its left side.

Instead of a card, Sponsors can advance the Break token that many steps and pay out that much money. The upgraded side plays several cards at once and may take cards from the display for a folder-number fee.

The slice says upgraded Sponsors may play cards with a certain icon condition, but the extraction lost the symbol, so the condition itself does not appear here.

The Meerkat Den example shows the pattern. It needs strength 5 and reputation 3, sits next to rock, and its own herbivore icon pays 2 Appeal at once.

Three Throttles and a Burst Valve

Association throttles you three ways at once: workers, strength, and repeat visits.

  • Workers cap how often you can return to a favourite task.
  • Strength caps which tasks you can afford this turn.
  • Breaks reset both, so timing your big Association turn matters.

Reputation is a gate, not a score. It widens your display reach and unlocks stronger Sponsor cards.

Partner zoos and universities are discounts that double as icons. They cheapen future Animals while feeding the icon counts that conservation projects demand, as the rules discussion also shows.

Conservation work is the conversion step. Map tokens become track progress, and the freed map bonuses pay you back immediately.

Sponsors is the burst valve. One strong action becomes one strong card, or steady money toward the next break.
