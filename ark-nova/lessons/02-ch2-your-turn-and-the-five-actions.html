Ark Nova: The Short Version
Aa

Your Turn and the Five Actions

Ark Nova has no formal rounds. Players simply take turns in clockwise order, one action per turn, until the end of the game is triggered, as the Ark Nova rulebook (Capstone Games, English) describes.

Between turns there are breaks, when play pauses for an administration phase. During a break you discard down to your hand limit, recall workers, and gain income, among other things. One break-timing edge case appears in the official FAQ V2 EN:

"You perform the steps 1-5 of the break. Only step 6 (returning the break token) does not happen."

The Five Actions

You perform your one action with five action cards sitting below your zoo map. Here is what each one does at a high level:

  • Cards: gain new Zoo cards, meaning Animals, Sponsor, and Conservation Project cards, from the deck or the display.
  • Build: build standard enclosures for animals of all categories, special enclosures for particular categories, plus kiosks and pavilions.
  • Animals: add animals to empty enclosures. You must meet special conditions and pay the acquisition cost. In return animals give you special abilities and icons for your zoo, which you may need to support conservation projects.
  • Association: send association workers to zoological associations. With their help your reputation increases, and you can gain partner zoos and universities from other continents, support conservation projects, or donate to animal conservation.
  • Sponsors: play sponsors into your zoo. They either support you directly or fund a project such as a unique building. Many give an ongoing effect or income, and some gain you extra appeal or conservation points at the end of the game.

The rulebook slice states this five-way split and points to later pages for the full detail of each action.

If you cannot or do not want to perform any of the five, there is an alternative. The X-token action gains you exactly one X-token instead of a normal action. You still select a card and move it to slot 1, shifting the rest right, but the slot strength does nothing for this action.

"You may not 'do nothing' with your action."

The extracted rulebook text drops the number in the sentence that describes the strength at which you perform the chosen action, and the upgrade icon for card upgrades does not survive extraction. Upgrades are therefore described here only as flipping a card from side I to side II, which stays improved for the rest of the game.

Slots and Strength

Each action card sits in a numbered slot, and the slot sets the strength. The numbers run from 1 on the far left to 5 on the far right, so the further right a card sits when you use it, the more powerful the action.

Using an action resets it. You shift the chosen card down to mark it in use, perform it at that slot's strength, then remove it and slide every card left of the empty slot one step right. The used card goes into the now-empty slot 1. The shift works the same in both directions, per the same official FAQ:

"If you place the Action card in position 1, cards that were previously to the left of the Action card move 1 position to the right. If you place the Action card in position 5, cards that were previously to the right of the Action card move 1 position to the left."
  • Popular actions keep dropping back to slot 1, so they stay weak.
  • Ignored actions drift right on their own, so they grow stronger without you spending anything.
  • The worked example: take Build from slot 4, perform it at strength 4, then move the cards from slots 1 to 3 one step right and place Build into slot 1.

X-tokens bend this curve. Each token spent adds 1 to the strength, and you may spend as many as you like, though some actions stop improving past strength 5. Two timing rules matter: you can never hold more than 5 X-tokens, and you set the strength before performing the action, so tokens gained mid-action cannot boost the action already underway; this matches the rulebook. On multiplier timing the official FAQ is explicit:

"No. You must immediately place the multiplier token on any of your Action cards."

Two finishing touches round out the loop. Some Zoo cards trigger an effect after finishing your action, and those resolve only after the action is complete, including moving the cards. And when you earn a card upgrade, you flip one action card of your choice from side I to side II in its current slot, unlocking the improved version from your next action on.
