Animal Card Layout

Money costBelow the enclosure needsPrice paid before placing. A partner zoo on the home continent cuts 3 money per continent icon.

Enclosure Sizes

EnclosureSizeNotes
Standard enclosure1 to 5Built empty side up with a yellow edge. One animal each, then flipped to the occupied side.
Petting Zoo3One per zoo. Only petting zoo animals fit here, never a standard tile.
Large Bird Aviary5One per zoo. Needs the upgraded Build action. Holds some birds beside standard tiles.
Reptile House5One per zoo. Needs the upgraded Build action. Holds reptiles beside standard tiles.

Every built space costs 2 money, so cost equals size times two. Special houses never flip; used room is marked with player tokens instead.

Requirement Icons

IconWhat It Means
Size hexagonMinimum enclosure size the animal accepts.
Rock or water adjacencyEnclosure must border that many rock or water spaces. The demand follows the animal into a special house too.
ContinentAnimal origin. Partner zoos discount its cost and its icon feeds conservation projects.
CategoryOne of the seven animal kinds below. Projects and effects ask for these.
Partner zoo demandYou must already hold that partner before playing the animal.
Special house demandThe animal goes only in the named special house.
Way of life and biome marksNot covered by this course's source.
Placement bonus marksNot covered by this course's source. The slices point to the printed overview for their meanings.

CategoryNotes

BearA subcategory shown black on white. It mostly appears beside a herbivore or predator mark.
Petting Zoo animalShown black on white. Replaces the biological category and fits only the Petting Zoo.
