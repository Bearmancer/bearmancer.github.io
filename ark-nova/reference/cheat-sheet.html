The Goal in Two Sentences

You build a modern zoo, raising appeal with animals and conservation with projects. The appeal and conservation counters move toward each other, and the player with the best combination of the two wins.

The Turn Loop

There are no formal rounds. On your turn you pick one of your five action cards, perform it at the strength of its slot, then move that card to slot 1 and shift the rest up. Cards you ignore drift right on their own and grow stronger until you use them.

The Five Actions

ActionWhat It Buys You
CardsNew zoo cards, meaning animals, sponsors, and conservation projects, from the deck or the display.
BuildEmpty homes for later animals: standard enclosures, special enclosures, plus kiosks and pavilions.
AnimalsResidents for empty enclosures. Pay the cost, meet every condition, and gain appeal with icons for projects.
AssociationWorker tasks that raise reputation, gain partner zoos and universities, or support conservation projects.
SponsorsSponsor cards played into your zoo for direct help, funded buildings, ongoing income, or late points.

The Power Mechanic

Slots run from 1 on the far left to 5 on the far right, so the further right a card sits, the stronger its action. Each X-token spent adds 1 strength, set before the action starts, and you can never hold more than 5.

Scoring in Brief

Appeal pays money at every break and conservation unlocks rewards along the way. When your two counters land in the same scoring area or pass one another, the end of the game is triggered and every other player takes one more turn. The final score comes out of the pair: your conservation area sets a target number and your appeal is measured against it, so uncrossed counters cannot score positive.

Card Randomness in Three Lines

  • One deck holds three kinds of cards: animals, sponsors, and conservation projects.
  • Six face-up display cards sit in numbered folders beside your hand, and your reputation decides which folders you can take from.
  • A strong Cards action can snap exactly one display card regardless of range, and the display refills only after your turn ends.

First Game: Do This

  • Build the empty home one turn before the animal arrives.
  • Build on placement bonus marks so the bonus funds your next building.
  • Move both counters every game, since appeal without conservation cannot win.
  • Watch the display and raise reputation to reach more of it.
  • Spend X-tokens to rescue a weak slot instead of waiting for it to drift right.
