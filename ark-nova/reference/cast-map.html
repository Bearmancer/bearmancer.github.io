The cast here is the game's recurring pieces, not people: the actors that show up in every chapter and do something.

The Five Actions

Cards — The gatherer. It draws new zoo cards from the deck or the display while pushing the shared break clock two steps forward.

Build — The builder. It raises empty enclosures, kiosks, and pavilions on the zoo map, paying 2 money per space before placing.

Animals — The resident-maker. It fills empty enclosures with appeal-earning animals, checking each card's conditions, paying its cost, and firing its effects.

Association — The slow engine. It spends workers on the shared board for reputation, partner zoos, partner universities, conservation project work, and, once upgraded, donations.

Sponsors — The burst valve. It turns action strength into played sponsor cards, or, instead of a card, into break-advancing money.

The Fallback Move

X-Token Action — The fallback move. When no action suits the turn, it trades the turn for exactly 1 X-token, moving a card to slot 1 with no help from slot strength.

The Two Counters

Appeal Counter — The earner. It climbs the appeal track as animals arrive and sets the money collected at every break.

Conservation Counter — The climber on the opposite track. It advances through supported projects and donations, unlocking one-time rewards along the way.

The Meeting — The ending both counters race toward. Once they share a scoring area or pass one another, the end of the game is triggered.

The Shared Table

Display — The public window. Its six face-up cards in numbered folders sit within reach of high-reputation zoos and refill at the end of each turn.

Association Worker — The staffer. It is posted from the zoo map onto board tasks, costs extra on repeat visits, and comes home at every break.

Break — The pause. It trims hands to the limit, clears card tokens, returns workers, refills the display and partners, pays income, and restarts the clock.
