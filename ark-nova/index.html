Ark Nova: The Short Version

A nine-chapter course on the board game Ark Nova, built for a first play. Glossary · Cast Map · Icon Reference · Cheat Sheet

Ark Nova is a big box with a big rulebook. This course keeps the parts you need to sit down and finish a first game: the loop, the five actions, what actually scores — including the 2023 scoring update — the setup table, the player-count differences and solo rules, and how to live with a shuffled deck.

Chapters

  • 1. The Whole Game on One Page — The mental model, and what ends the game.
  • 2. Your Turn and the Five Actions — The loop, the five action cards, and why they get stronger.
  • 3. Cards: The Real Bottleneck — The deck, the display, and the reputation track.
  • 4. Building Your Zoo — Enclosures, animals, and what an animal asks for.
  • 5. Association, Sponsors, Reputation — Workers, projects, and the road to conservation points.
  • 6. Scoring, Flatlined — How you actually win, in the fewest moving parts.
  • 7. At the Table: Setup, Player Counts, Solo — The setup procedure, what changes by player count, and the solo game.
  • 8. Playing the Hand You're Dealt — What is random, what is not, and what to do about it.
  • 9. Marine Worlds in Five Minutes — The expansion, aquariums, and its rule updates.

Reference

  • Glossary — Canonical terms and names as of chapter 9.
  • Cast Map — The game's recurring pieces and what each one does.
  • Icon Reference — The symbols on animal and enclosure cards.
  • Cheat Sheet — The whole game on one printable page.
