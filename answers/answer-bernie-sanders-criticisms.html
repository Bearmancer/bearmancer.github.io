Documented Criticisms of Bernie Sanders Beyond Politics

Beyond policy disagreement, the verified record supports criticisms on five fronts — the accuracy of his headline claims about health spending, his gun votes set against his rhetoric, the VA oversight he said he should have done better, the way his 2019 heart attack was disclosed, and reported episodes involving the Clinton data breach and Elizabeth Warren; here 'legitimate criticism' is read as a charge evidenced in verifiable records rather than a difference of political outlook.123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930313233343536373839404142434445464748495051

The fact-check record

PolitiFact's running scorecard for Bernie Sanders lists 64 fact-checks rated Mostly True and 19 rated False.5253

The same scorecard lists no check rated Pants on Fire.54

It also lists 21 checks rated True, 49 rated Half True and 28 rated Mostly False.55

PolitiFact describes the subject of these checks as the senator from Vermont who represented the state's At-Large House district from 1991 until 2007.56

Among the individual statements PolitiFact examined was his February 2020 debate claim that a study found Medicare for All would lower US health costs by $450 billion a year and save 68,000 lives.57

PolitiFact also checked his December 11, 2022 claim on CNN that 85 million Americans have no health insurance, and a March 11, 2020 television-ad claim that Joe Biden has advocated cutting Social Security for 40 years.5859

The list continues into recent years, including a June 12, 2026 X post claiming Elon Musk pays the same amount into Social Security as someone making $184,500.60

It also includes a claim in his March 5, 2020 NBC interview that he voted against the Brady Bill five times for background checks.61

Health spending claims

Sanders repeated in 2019 — on Twitter, in a CBS interview and in a June CNN appearance — that the US spends twice as much per capita on health care as any other country.12345678

What he could have said accurately, on OECD figures of an estimated $3,992 per person in 2018, is that the US spends more than twice the average for OECD countries.9

The gun vote record

On the gun record, House Clerk roll calls, PolitiFact and Vox agree that Sanders voted against the Brady Bill in 1993, the law that imposed a five-day waiting period before a handgun could be sold.10111213

Fourteen years after supporting the 2005 law that protects gun makers and sellers from liability lawsuits, Sanders co-sponsored a bill to reverse it.6263

Veterans Affairs oversight

As chairman of the Senate Veterans' Affairs Committee from 2013 to 2015, Sanders was responsible for oversight while the backlog for medical appointments at VA hospitals grew.142122232425

In 2016 he conceded the criticism himself, saying of his time overseeing the VA: 'We should have done better.'14151617181920

The heart attack disclosure

On his October 2019 heart attack, his aides revealed on the Wednesday that he had had chest pains the previous evening and that doctors inserted two stents to address a blockage in an artery.262728293031323334

Doctors at Desert Springs Hospital Medical Center in Las Vegas said he was diagnosed with a heart attack after going to an outside facility and being transferred to their hospital.323334

Data breach and the Warren meeting

In December 2015 a data breach let the Sanders campaign see the Clinton campaign's voter files, and the Democratic National Committee barred Sanders from its voter database.3536373839

CNN reported that when Sanders and Elizabeth Warren met at Warren's Washington apartment in December 2018, Sanders responded that he did not believe a woman could win.404142434445464748495051

Counterweight: legislative effectiveness

Against the effectiveness charge, between 1995 and 2005 no member of the House got more of their amendments approved by roll-call vote than Sanders, whom Rolling Stone dubbed the 'amendment king.'646566
