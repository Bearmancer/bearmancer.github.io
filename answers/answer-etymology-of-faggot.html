Etymology of faggot

2026-10-04

This hurtful word started as a normal word for firewood. In the late 1200s English took Old French fagot, a bundle of sticks for fire. Only in the early 1900s did American slang turn it into a slur for gay men.

Just wood

In 1296 a boss told his worker, the reeve, to buy wood and 500 fagots and bring them fast to Westminster, as the Wordorigins history shows from old abbey papers. About 1312 a list counts 96 faggots for 18 pence. The Online Etymology Dictionary says the French word likely comes from Italian fagotto and Latin fascis, a bundle. Around 1300 in English it meant only sticks for fuel, with no link to burnings, as the Grammarphobia summary explains.

Fire and church court

Because sticks feed fire, fire and faggot became short words for church punishment from the 1550s. In about 1555 Hugh Latimer wrote of people fleeing for fear of the fagot, meaning the wood, not the people. People who said sorry had to wear a small fagot sign on the arm. The link to fire is real, but it never named the victim.

From heavy load to rude word for woman

By 1591 Thomas Lodge used faggot as a rude word for a woman, the first such use in the Oxford English Dictionary, kept in the Fordham copy of the Oxford English Dictionary entry. The idea was likely heavy load, like baggage for a woman seen as a burden. Once used for woman, it could shift to men seen as soft, like queen or fairy.

American slang in 1910s

A 1913 letter by John Reed already uses it for a drag show. The 1914 slang book gives the first printed use, all the fagots will be dressed in drag, meaning sissies. The NPR retelling notes the same 1914 ball and a 1937 book use, and says the last step is still debated.

What is not true

The stake story is false. It says gay men were called faggots because they were burned on sticks, yet burnings were for church crimes, not usually for gay acts, the slur starts in America long after burnings stop, and England ordered hanging, not burning, from 1533. Yiddish feygele, little bird, shows as gay slang only in 1967, after English use, so it copies English. School fag for a young helper comes from to fag, to work hard, and was not used in America then. The true path is bundle to load to woman to soft man.
