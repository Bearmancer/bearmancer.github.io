Does Putin Speak English?

2026-10-02

No — Vladimir Putin does speak some English, though he rarely uses it in public. He delivered an entire bid speech in English for the Kremlin in Guatemala in 2007, and his office says he understands the language well enough to correct interpreters, as reported by iNews.

Wrong

  1. wrong

    "Vladimir Putin does not speak English."

    The official transcript of his 2007 Olympic bid address is in English throughout, opening with his address to the committee, per the Kremlin. His spokesman says he understands English almost completely and sometimes corrects translators, while NBC News describes him as known to speak some English yet communicating through an interpreter in negotiations. The reason he sticks to Russian in formal settings is comfort and protocol, not inability: his English is serviceable but not fluent, so he uses a translator when precision matters, per iNews.
