3. Narrative

The seating. Deputies for revolution sat left of the chair at Versailles. Deputies for the king sat right (Britannica).

"those who were loyal to religion and the king took up positions to the right of the chair"

Baron de Gauville wrote that line. The wording is confirmed against the record.1 Left became the party of movement. Right became the party of order. The labels took decades to harden (TIME).

The tool. Read every position on two axes. Economics runs from equality to hierarchy. Authority runs from libertarian to authoritarian (Political Spectrum). The center is positional. It moves as the poles move.

The American translation. The split reads as progressivism versus conservatism. The middle decides elections. Gallup 2024 found 37 percent conservative, 34 percent moderate, 25 percent liberal (Gallup). Party sorting hit records in the same tables, confirmed against them1: 77 percent of Republicans conservative and 55 percent of Democrats liberal.

Confusion one: liberalism. Classical liberals limit the state to protect autonomy. Modern American liberals use the state for equality, sitting center-left (Classical Liberalism). Same word, opposite instructions for the state.

Confusion two: three market words. Keep these apart:

  • Libertarianism puts liberty first and rejects state paternalism over personal choice (Cato Mission).
  • Neoliberalism reorganizes state power to expand markets (Naked Keynesianism). It uses the state that libertarianism distrusts.
  • Neoclassical economics is an analytical school, not a project. Its marginalist efficiency logic serves neoliberals, but many of its economists defend market-failure policy. The Smith rhetoric covers marginalist math, confirmed against the same distinction.1

The history fits one sentence. The 1938 Lippmann colloquium seeded the project, the Mont Pelerin Society formed in 1947 (Taylor and Francis), and Chicago supplied monetarism and deregulation (Neoliberalism).

Confusion three: three left words. Keep these apart:

  • Progressivism seeks progress beyond the status quo toward just conditions (Center for American Progress). It rose against industrial misery between 1890 and 1920, steering between European socialism and hands-off conservatism.1 Its line runs from the Progressive Era through the New Deal and Great Society to the Green New Deal. Modern faces include Warren alongside Sanders, Ocasio-Cortez, and Mamdani (Britannica, 21st-century progressivism).
  • Social democracy keeps capitalism and adds safety nets. Democratic socialism replaces capitalism with worker and community ownership (Good Party). Reform versus transform.
  • The Nordic model is a mixed market plus universal welfare, born of a 1930s compromise (Nordic Model). It is social democracy working, not socialism installed.

Attachments, not ideologies. Populism is a thin frame of pure people versus corrupt elite. It needs a host, left or right (Mudde). Trump pairs it with nationalism. Sanders pairs it with socialism. Fusionism married libertarian means to traditionalist ends in 1962 and powered the Reagan coalition (National Review).

Worked example: DSA. Apply the three tests from the next section. Two axes: left on economics, libertarian-leaning on authority. Reform test: transform, so democratic socialist, not social democratic. Host test: none needed, since democratic socialism is already a full ideology. The organization merged in 1982 and stayed multi-tendency (DSA History). It held near 5,000 for decades, surged past 79,000 in 2021, fell to 51,000 by October 2024 (State of DSA), then climbed to 92,912 by December 2025 (City and State). Counts after 2021 stay date-pinned, since they move with campaigns. The DSA record here is the source account, unfindable beyond first-party pages.

4. Machinery

Three tests place any position. Each test returns one label.

Two-axis test. Score economics from equality to hierarchy. Score authority from libertarian to authoritarian. DSA lands left-libertarian. A police-state nationalist lands right-authoritarian.

Reform test. Ask what it does with capitalism. Safety nets on a kept market mean social democrat. Worker ownership replacing the market means democratic socialist.

Host test. Ask whether the label can stand alone. Populism never travels alone. Name its host: nationalism on the right, socialism on the left.
