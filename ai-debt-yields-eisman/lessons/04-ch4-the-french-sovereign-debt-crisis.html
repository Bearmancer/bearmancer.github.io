The French Sovereign Debt Crisis

France's debt-to-GDP ratio reaching 120% pushed sovereign bond spreads over German Bunds to 175 basis points, their widest risk premium since the 2011 crisis.12

What the French Sovereign Debt Crisis Is

France's debt-to-GDP ratio has reached 120%.12

The spread between French 10-year sovereign bonds and German 10-year Bunds widened by 16 basis points to 175 basis points.12

Why the French Spread Widened

The risk premium on French 10-year sovereign bonds reached its highest level since the 2011 European sovereign debt crisis.12
