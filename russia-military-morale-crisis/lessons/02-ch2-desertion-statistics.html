Desertion Statistics

Russia publishes no deserter count, and the narration reads that silence as fear of handing the West a propaganda victory. The secrecy is plausible, but the motive reading is the narration's own.

Convictions: The Floor

The 28,000 convictions since the invasion began are Meduza's reported court tally. Because it counts only soldiers who were caught, it is a floor for desertion, never a total.

The UN Estimate: The Relay

The 50,000 figure is a relay of the UN estimate through the Novaya Gazeta write-up of the UN findings. The "almost double the convicts" arithmetic holds on those inputs. Figures of 16,000 and 13,500 sometimes attached to this debate are misattributed cumulative and all-service counts, per the same UN relay record.

Frontelligence: The Projection

The 70,000-plus figure is a Frontelligence Insight projection of voluntary departures by year-end 2025, not a counted total. The attached "almost 10 percent of deployed forces" depends on which deployed-force denominator is used. The video's 200,000 for Ukrainian unauthorized absence sits above the documented 140,000 to 180,000 range in the same desertion-data record.

Floor, Relay, Projection

  • Convictions anchor the floor: only the caught, only the sentenced.
  • The UN relay scales it up: an estimate with methodology attached.
  • The projection extends it forward: a year-end scenario, never a census.

The three rungs never collapse into one number. Each answers a different question, and only the first is a count.
