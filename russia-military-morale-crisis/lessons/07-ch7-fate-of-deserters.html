4. Machinery

The chapter works by closing every exit in sequence.

  • Europe: pushbacks and single-digit asylum odds.
  • The border: helicopters, phone records, and relatives as leverage.
  • Capture: trial or disappearance, with return to the front as the best case.

The course-level revision stands here. The direction of the morale-crisis account is supported across independent lines, but its certainty is systematically overstated, so the whole reads as partially correct: true in tendency, biased in confidence.
