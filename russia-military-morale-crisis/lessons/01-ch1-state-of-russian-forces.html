The State of Russian Forces

The Russian army hits hard and blunders often: capable of extraordinary violence, prone to senseless mistakes — the combination behind the central claim of brittleness.

"Moscow throws waves of people to their deaths as 'sponges' for bullets and drones, hoping to wear down Ukraine's defenses."

"Meat assaults" describes waves driven forward to absorb fire and wear down Ukrainian defenses — coercive attacks that spend lives to probe lines.

The Sadanikovs' Escape

The Sadanikovs hid for months, then crossed the Black Sea toward Turkey through storms — the same flight told in the RFE/RL deserter's-odyssey account, down to the age detail. He was on his third tour, with Wagner time behind him; surname spellings differ between tellings.

  • Hundreds of kilometers of rough seas, days without food.
  • The stated motive: front-line horrors, plus abuses faced by women in service.
  • An escape that drew worldwide attention.
