4. Machinery

The chapter works by escalation: past reputation against present clips, then anecdotes argued as system.

  • Superlative as hook: the opening boast sets up the fall.
  • Pattern plus anecdote: supported patterns carry the weight that single stories cannot.
  • Inflation as bias: where the record gives a pattern, the narration claims a collapse.
