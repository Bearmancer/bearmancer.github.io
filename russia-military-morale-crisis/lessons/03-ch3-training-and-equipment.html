4. Machinery

The chapter works by variance: three training lengths that must not be averaged.

  • Five days: one recruit's story, the sharpest number, the weakest base.
  • Ten to fifteen days: documented short courses for assault-role troops.
  • Four months: the legal baseline that both fall short of.

Equipment losses then compound the training gap, since untaught crews break what little kit arrives. The rebuild pressure behind that spiral is the same 2 decade-long projection.
