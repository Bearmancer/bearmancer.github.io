Context and Offstage Voices

Chapters 2 and 6 carry no on-stage cast: they argue through counts, outlets, and unnamed command structures rather than named actors. Outlet voices such as Meduza, Verstka, and Frontelligence Insight appear only as attributed sources inside those chapters, so they hold glossary entries but no roster rows. Figures whose names the captions garble, notably Rozsypne for the Zaitsevo facility and Novorengoy for the gas plants, are tabled under their corrected forms here until a record check settles them.
