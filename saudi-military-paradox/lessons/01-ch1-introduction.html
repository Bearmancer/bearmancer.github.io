Introduction

Wealth usually buys power, and few states test that rule like Saudi Arabia. Its crown prince, Mohammed bin Salman, directs wealth and military policy.

"Across the armies of the world, wealth is power."

The video opens with riches, including a sovereign fund above a trillion dollars, then asks why the arsenal lags behind the money.

How Much Saudi Arabia Spends

The 2024 actual spending was about 80 billion dollars, seventh worldwide, at roughly seven percent of output.

Later figures moved the rank, with SIPRI recording 83.2 billion dollars, eighth place, and six and a half percent.

Saudi outlays sit in the same band as Germany, India, Britain, and France in the 2024 ranking.

  • Big money is real, so the puzzle is real.
  • Rankings shift by year, so dates matter.
  • Spending peers build more at home.

Where the Money Goes

Oil revenue flows into the state budget, and the budget feeds one of the largest military outlays on earth.

Yet the regional return looks thin: the kingdom ranks fifth in the Middle East and twenty-fifth worldwide on the firepower index despite leading regional spending.

Scholarship frames the gap as design, not accident: Gulf rulers traded combat power for regime safety, accepting forces that are more hapless but coup-resistant.
