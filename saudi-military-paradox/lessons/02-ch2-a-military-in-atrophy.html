Troops and Spending

"The nation boasts a total active duty troop count of 260,000."

Derived tables put the figure at about 257,000 active troops plus reserves, close to the video's round number.

On spending, the 2024 actual was near 80 billion dollars at about seven percent of output, ranked seventh.

The standing army slice is about seventy-five thousand soldiers.

The Split Between Army and Guard

"Over half belong to the Saudi Arabian National Guard."

The Guard holds roughly one hundred thousand to one hundred thirty thousand of a quarter-million-strong force, which is under a separate ministry but below half.

"Its military is split in half for no clear reason at all."

The reason is clear: parallel militaries are textbook coup-proofing, a deliberate regime-safety design per coup-proofing scholarship. The Guard's core duty is shielding the ruling family against coups.

Two parallel ground structures divide the manpower: the regular army under the defense ministry and the Guard under its own ministry. The split weakens combined warfighting but strengthens regime survival, which is why rulers keep it.

Regional Comparisons

  • Turkey fields about four hundred eighty-one thousand active and three hundred eighty thousand reserve.
  • Iran fields about five hundred seventy thousand active and three hundred fifty thousand reserve.
  • Egypt fields about four hundred thirty-eight thousand active and four hundred seventy-nine thousand reserve on under five billion dollars.
  • Israel and Iraq sit in the same order of magnitude but clearly smaller: Israel holds about one hundred sixty-nine thousand active troops and Iraq about one hundred ninety-three thousand.

Foreign Trainers and Foreign Hardware

Harsh foreign assessments of Saudi forces are documented, from a 1991 coalition commander calling Saudi ground units the weakest with inept commanders. No survey of foreign trainers exists, so the wider claim about most trainers reaches beyond this one account.

Hardware flows inward from abroad: tanks count near several hundred modernized Abrams, artillery and rocket fleets number in the hundreds per inventory tables, and the air fleet blends American strike jets with over one hundred fifty European fighters.

Air defenses rest on a dedicated branch plus new foreign surface-to-air buys such as the Korean missile deal.

The system logic is import, duplicate, and divide: buy top-shelf kit abroad, overlap suppliers for political cover, and keep parallel chains loyal to the throne.
